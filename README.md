# Real World Ready Coach

> A folder-based AI coach for young adults ages 17–24 who are leaving the nest and navigating real life for the first time.

---

## What This Is

Real World Ready Coach is a personal AI coach you drop into a Claude Project.

It coaches young adults through the real-world milestones nobody teaches you in school:

- Where your money comes from and how to protect it
- Opening a bank account and building a budget
- Grocery shopping and meal planning
- Renting your first apartment
- Buying and maintaining a car
- Preparing for job interviews
- Reviewing leases, loan documents, bills, and test results
- Understanding credit scores
- Scheduling doctor appointments
- Taking care of your mental health while doing all of the above

This is not a knowledge base. It is a coach.

It asks questions. It listens. It pushes back when you are about to make a mistake. It helps you think clearly and gives you a next step.

---

## The 7 Branches

The coach guides you through 7 branches — from overwhelmed to ready.

```
BRANCH 1 — Still in the Nest       No plan. No budget. Dependent on others.
BRANCH 2 — Money In                Where does your money come from? First paycheck.
BRANCH 3 — Money Managed           Bank account. Budget. Direct deposit. No overdrafts.
BRANCH 4 — Daily Independence      Groceries. Appointments. Bills. First credit card.
BRANCH 5 — Bigger Decisions        Apartment. Car. Lease review. Loan review. Credit score.
BRANCH 6 — Career Ready            Job interviews. Job offers. Document review.
BRANCH 7 — Soaring                 Emergency savings. Stable budget. Ready for anything.
```

## How to Use It in 3 Steps

**STEP 1**
Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) — it is free. Create an account.

**STEP 2**
Click **Projects** then **New Project**.
Name it anything you want.
Upload all the files from this folder.

**STEP 3**
Start talking to your coach. Type this to begin:

> "I just graduated. I don't know where to start."

The coach takes it from there.

## What Good Coaching Looks Like

**Bad response:**
> Here are 10 budgeting tips.

**Good response:**
> Before I give you a plan, tell me this: how much money comes in each month, what bills do you already have, and what is one money habit you know is hurting you right now?

**Bad response:**
> A lease is a legal contract between a landlord and tenant.

**Good response:**
> I can help you review this lease for possible red flags, but I cannot tell you whether it is legally valid. Let's look at the rent amount, fees, maintenance responsibilities, and anything that could cost you money later.

## Document Review Mode

Upload or paste any important document and the coach will help you review it before you sign, pay, or agree.

Supported documents:
- Apartment leases
- Car loan paperwork
- Credit card offers
- Bank account disclosures
- Job offer letters
- Medical test results
- Bills
- Repair estimates
- Financial aid letters
- Insurance documents

The coach helps you understand the document, spot possible red flags, and prepare smart questions. It is not a lawyer or doctor — it is a coach helping you slow down.

## Safety and Boundaries

This coach does not provide legal, medical, tax, financial, lending, insurance, or mental health therapy.

It helps you understand, prepare questions, spot red flags, and plan next steps. It recommends a qualified professional whenever the issue is serious, urgent, or outside basic coaching.

---

## The Goal

Help young adults build confidence by making their next adult decision clearer, safer, smaller, and more manageable.

One branch at a time.
