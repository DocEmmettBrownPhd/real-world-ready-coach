Setup Guide

How to Set It Up

This coach runs inside Claude Projects. Here is exactly how to set it up, step by step.

First: which account do you have?

Free Account
Claude.ai Free
Free accounts can use Claude but may not have access to Projects depending on your region. If you do not see “Projects” in the sidebar after signing in, you will need to upgrade to Pro.
Recommended
Claude.ai Pro — $20/month
Pro accounts have full access to Projects. This is what the coach is built for. Projects let the coach remember all the files and stay in character across every conversation.
1

Download the coach folder

Click the button below to download the coach as a ZIP file. It contains all the files the coach needs. You will upload them in Step 4.

⇓ Download Coach Folder
After downloading: Find the ZIP file in your Downloads folder. Unzip it (double-click on Mac, right-click → Extract All on Windows). You will see a folder called real-world-ready-coach-main. Keep that folder open — you will need it in Step 4.
2

Create a Claude account

Go to claude.ai and create an account. It takes about two minutes. Use your email address.

Already have an account? Skip to Step 3.
3

Make sure you have access to Projects

After signing in, look at the left sidebar. You should see a section called Projects. If you do not see it, you will need a Pro account.

To upgrade: go to claude.ai/settings/billing and choose the Pro plan ($20/month). You can cancel anytime.

What you are looking for: In the left sidebar after signing in, there should be a “Projects” section with a “+ New project” button. If you see it, you are ready for Step 4.
4

Create a new Project

In the left sidebar, click Projects then click + New project. Name it anything you want — something like My Coach or Real World Ready works fine.

What this does: A Project is a special space in Claude where you can upload files that Claude reads before every conversation. This is how the coach knows who it is and how to talk to you.
5

Upload the coach files

Inside your new Project, you will see a section called Project knowledge or Add content. Click it to upload files.

Upload these files from the folder you downloaded in Step 1:

Upload all of these files:
• identity.md
• rules.md
• examples.md
• onboarding.md
• source.md
• All 10 files inside the reference folder

You do not need to upload README.md or submission.md — those are for reference only.

6

Start your first conversation

Click Start chat inside the Project. The coach will introduce itself, explain what it does, and ask you a few questions to find out where you are starting from.

You do not need to say anything special. Just type something like:

“I just graduated. I don’t really know where to start with money or any of this stuff.”

The coach takes it from there. It will ask you questions before it gives you any advice.

✓

You are set up

Every conversation you start inside this Project will have the full coach loaded. The coach will ask questions, listen, and give you one clear next step at a time. It will remember what you tell it within each conversation.

Sharing with someone else: Send them the download link or email them the files. Then send them this page. They follow the same six steps above.

Mental Health

The Coach Checks In on You, Not Just Your Budget

Adult life is a lot to carry. The coach weaves mental health into every conversation — it is not a bolt-on section at the end.

❤

Overwhelmed is normal

You are not failing. You are starting. The coach knows the difference.

🏃

Movement matters

20 minutes of walking affects every decision you make that day. The coach will remind you.

💬

Therapy is a tool

Not a last resort. The coach knows how to find low-cost options including free EAP sessions through many employers.

🌞

Rest is not optional

Sleep affects financial decisions, interviews, and relationships. The coach treats it like any other necessity.

🎮

Fun is required

Recreation is not a reward for finishing everything else. The coach helps you protect space for it.
