What It Does

Everything Adult Life Throws at You

Here is exactly what this coach helps with — and why each piece matters.

🌿

Branch-by-Branch Coaching

You do not have to figure out where to start. The coach asks you a few questions, finds out where you actually are in life, and starts from there. It walks you through 7 stages — from having no plan to being fully on your feet. You move at your own pace. Nobody rushes you.
For parentsThis gives your graduate a structured path that starts where they are, not where you wish they were. Each stage builds on the last.
📈

Investing — Start Small, Start Now

You do not need a lot of money to start investing. You need time — and you have more of it than anyone. The coach walks you through opening a Robinhood account, explains what an index fund is in plain English, and shows you how putting just 5% of your paycheck into an S&P 500 fund can grow into tens of thousands of dollars by the time you are 50. No stock picking. No gambling. Just the habit.
For parentsThe coach introduces investing at exactly the right moment — after the emergency fund is started, never before. It recommends index funds only, explains compound interest with real numbers, and builds the habit before lifestyle inflation sets in. This is the financial lesson most adults wish they had learned at 18.
📄

Document Review

About to sign a lease? Got a car loan offer? Received a medical bill you do not understand? Paste it or upload a photo and the coach reads it with you. It tells you in plain English what it says, what looks off, and what questions to ask before you agree to anything. It will not let you sign something you do not understand.
For parentsYoung adults sign things they do not fully read. This coach slows them down and flags problems before they become expensive mistakes. Leases, car loans, job offers, medical bills — all covered.
🔍

Gap Assessment

The coach does not assume you know nothing. In your first conversation, it asks what you already have — a bank account, a car, a job, health insurance, a lease. It maps out what you have covered and what is still missing. Then it focuses on the gaps, not the stuff you already figured out.
For parentsInstead of starting over from scratch, the coach finds out what your graduate already has in place and only coaches what is actually missing. No wasted time, no condescension.
🏉

Goal Tracking

The coach asks what you actually want — your own place, a car, a career job, financial breathing room. It connects your goals to a real plan. Every step you take is pointed at something you said you wanted, not a generic checklist someone else wrote.
For parentsThe coach ties your graduate’s own stated goals to the coaching plan. They are more likely to follow through on a plan they helped build.
📅

Progress Tracking & Check-Ins

You can check in any day by typing two words: Check in. The coach asks what you worked on, marks what you completed, notes what you did not get to, and gives you your next step. It keeps a running log so you can see how far you have come. You do not have to start over every time.
For parentsThe progress file shows a weekly log of what was accomplished and what was not. Your graduate can share it with you anytime. It is a real accountability tool, not just a conversation.
🧠

Built for Shy and Quiet Users

If you give short answers, say “I don’t know,” or go quiet when things feel awkward — the coach is built for that. It knows how to ask the question a different way, give you a range to pick from, or just wait. It never makes you feel dumb for not knowing something nobody taught you.
For parentsThe coaching rules are specifically designed for young adults who shut down under pressure. The coach pulls answers out gently, never pushes more than once on a hard topic, and always leads with a compliment before a correction.
❤

Mental Health Woven In

This is not just about money and leases. The coach checks in on how you are actually doing. It normalizes feeling overwhelmed. It talks about sleep, exercise, and therapy the same way it talks about budgets — as things that matter, not extras. If something feels too heavy, it notices and says so.
For parentsMental health is built into every stage, not a separate section. The coach treats stress, burnout, and anxiety as real parts of the adulting process and handles them the same way it handles financial decisions — practically and without judgment.
📚

Research-Backed Frameworks

Everything the coach knows comes from real sources — the CFPB, HUD, the FDIC, the USDA, and more. It is not just opinions. When it tells you how much rent you can afford, or what to look for in a car loan, or how to read a credit score, it is pulling from the same guidance that financial counselors use.
For parentsThe coach is built on 11 detailed reference frameworks covering every topic, all sourced from federal consumer protection agencies and established financial education research. It is not generic advice — it is structured, vetted guidance.

Setup Guide

How to Set It Up

Follow these steps exactly. Written for someone who has never used Claude before.

First — free or paid account?

Free
Claude Free Account
Free accounts let you chat with Claude. However, the Projects feature — which is what the coach requires — may not be available on free accounts in all regions. If you sign up and do not see “Projects” in the menu, you will need to upgrade.
Recommended — $20/month
Claude Pro Account
Pro includes full access to Projects. This is what the coach is built for. Projects let Claude read your uploaded files before every conversation so it stays in character as your personal coach. You can cancel anytime.
1

Download the coach folder

Click the button below. A ZIP file will download to your device.

⇓ Download Coach Folder
After downloading: Open your Downloads folder. Find the file called real-world-ready-coach-main.zip. Double-click it to unzip (Mac opens it automatically — on Windows, right-click and choose “Extract All”). You will now have a folder called real-world-ready-coach-main. Leave it open. You need it in Step 5.
2

Create a Claude account

Go to claude.ai or claude.com — both go to the same place. Click Sign up. Enter your email and create a password. Claude will send you a verification email — open it and click the link to confirm.

Already have an account? Sign in and skip to Step 3.
3

Check that you have access to Projects

After signing in, look at the left side of the screen. You will see a menu. Look for a section called Projects.

You see “Projects”: You are ready. Move to Step 4.

You do not see “Projects”: Click your name or account icon at the bottom left. Choose Upgrade to Pro and follow the steps. Cost is $20/month, cancel anytime. Once upgraded, come back and continue with Step 4.
4

Create a new Project

In the left menu, click Projects. Then click + New project or Create project.

A box will appear asking you to name the project. Type anything — for example:

My Coach  —  Real World Ready  —  Adulting Help

Click Create or press Enter. Your new Project will open.

What is a Project? A Project is a private space where you upload files. Claude reads those files before every conversation so it always knows who it is, what your goals are, and where you left off. Without the files it is just a regular chatbot. With them it is your personal coach.
5

Upload the coach files

Inside your Project, find the section called Project knowledge or Add content and click it. A file picker will open. Go to the real-world-ready-coach-main folder from Step 1 and upload these files:

identity.md   rules.md   examples.md   onboarding.md   progress.md   source.md

Then open the reference folder inside and upload all 11 files:
adulting-milestones.md   coaching-framework.md   budget-framework.md   banking-credit-framework.md   document-review-framework.md   car-buying-framework.md   renting-framework.md   job-interview-framework.md   grocery-framework.md   health-appointments-framework.md   investing-framework.md
Total: 17 files. 6 from the main folder + 11 from the reference folder. Do not upload README.md or submission.md.
6

Start your first conversation

Inside your Project, click Start chat or New conversation. Type something natural like:

“I just graduated. I don’t really know where to start with money or any of this adult stuff.”

The coach introduces itself, asks you questions to understand where you are starting from, and begins coaching. It always asks before it advises.

Important: Always start conversations from inside your Project — not from the main Claude chat screen. Starting from the main screen means the coach files are not loaded.
7

Check in any day to track your progress

After your first conversation, the coach gives you an updated progress.md file. Replace the one in your Project with the new version. This is how the coach remembers where you left off.

Any time you return, just type:

Check in

The coach reads your progress file, asks what you worked on, updates your tracker, and gives you your next step. Check in daily or whenever you want.

✓

You are set up

Every conversation inside this Project has the full coach loaded. It remembers your goals, your gaps, and where you left off — as long as you keep the progress file updated after each session.
