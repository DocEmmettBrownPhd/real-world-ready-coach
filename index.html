Setup Guide

How to Set It Up

Follow these steps exactly. This guide is written for someone who has never used Claude before.

First — do you need a free or paid account?

Free
Claude Free Account
Free accounts let you chat with Claude. However, the Projects feature — which is what the coach requires — may not be available on a free account in your region. If you sign up free and do not see “Projects” in the menu, you will need to upgrade.
Recommended — $20/month
Claude Pro Account
Pro accounts include full access to Projects. This is what the coach is built for. Projects let Claude read your uploaded files and stay in character as your coach across every conversation. You can cancel Pro at any time.
1

Download the coach folder to your device

Click the button below. It will download a ZIP file to your device — this is the coach folder.

⇓ Download Coach Folder
After it downloads: Open your Downloads folder. You will see a file called real-world-ready-coach-main.zip. Double-click it to unzip (on Mac it opens automatically — on Windows, right-click it and choose “Extract All”). You will now have a folder called real-world-ready-coach-main. Leave it open. You will need it later.
2

Create a Claude account

Go to claude.ai or claude.com — both go to the same place. Click Sign up.

Enter your email address and create a password. Claude will send you a verification email — open it and click the link to confirm your account.

Already have an account? Just sign in and skip to Step 3.
3

Check whether you have access to Projects

After signing in, look at the left side of the screen. You will see a sidebar menu. Look for a section called Projects.

If you see “Projects” in the menu: You are ready. Skip to Step 4.

If you do not see “Projects”: You need to upgrade to Pro. Click your name or account icon (bottom left), choose Upgrade to Pro, and follow the steps. Cost is $20/month and you can cancel anytime. Once upgraded, come back here and continue with Step 4.
4

Create a new Project

In the left sidebar, click Projects. Then click the button that says + New project or Create project.

A box will appear asking you to name your project. Type anything you want — for example:

My Coach   or   Real World Ready   or   Adulting Help

Then click Create or press Enter. Your new project will open.

What is a Project? A Project is a private space inside Claude where you upload files. Claude reads those files before every conversation, so it always knows who it is and what its job is. Without the files, Claude is just a general chatbot. With them, it becomes your personal coach.
5

Upload the coach files to your Project

Inside your new Project, look for a section on the right side (or top) that says Project knowledge or Add content. Click it.

A file picker will open. Navigate to the real-world-ready-coach-main folder you unzipped in Step 1. Upload these files:

identity.md
rules.md
examples.md
onboarding.md
progress.md
source.md
Then open the reference folder inside and upload all 10 files inside it: adulting-milestones.md   coaching-framework.md   budget-framework.md   banking-credit-framework.md   document-review-framework.md   car-buying-framework.md   renting-framework.md   job-interview-framework.md   grocery-framework.md   health-appointments-framework.md
Do not upload README.md or submission.md — those are informational only and not part of the coach.
Total files to upload: 16 files (6 in the main folder + 10 inside the reference folder).
6

Start your first conversation

Inside your Project, click the button that says Start chat or New conversation. A chat window will open.

You do not need to say anything special. Just type something natural, like:

“I just graduated. I don’t really know where to start with money or any of this adult stuff.”

The coach will introduce itself, ask you a few questions to understand where you are starting from, and then begin coaching. It asks before it advises.

Important: Always start conversations from inside your Project — not from the main Claude chat screen. Starting from the main screen means Claude does not have the coach files loaded and will behave like a regular chatbot.
7

Check in any day to track your progress

After your first conversation, the coach will give you an updated progress.md file. Replace the one in your Project with the new version. This is how the coach remembers where you are.

Any time you come back, just type:

Check in

The coach will read your progress file, ask what you worked on, update your tracker, and give you your next step. You can check in daily, weekly — whenever you want.

✓

You are set up

The coach is ready. Every conversation you start inside this Project will have everything loaded. It will remember your goals, your gaps, and where you left off — as long as you keep the progress file updated.
