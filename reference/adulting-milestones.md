# Adulting Milestones

This file lists the core milestones Real World Ready Coach helps users work through, organized by branch.

---

## Branch 1 — Still in the Nest
- Recognizing the need for a plan
- Identifying what feels most overwhelming
- Understanding that not knowing is okay — starting is the move

## Branch 2 — Money In
- Identifying income source: parents, allowance, part-time job, or first career job
- Understanding gross pay vs take-home pay
- Reading a pay stub
- Understanding pay schedules: weekly, biweekly, monthly
- Knowing when money arrives and planning around it

## Branch 3 — Money Managed
- Opening a checking account
- Opening a savings account
- Understanding bank fees and how to avoid them
- Setting up direct deposit
- Building a first budget based on real take-home pay
- Separating needs, wants, and obligations
- Avoiding overdrafts
- Starting an emergency fund (even small)
- Setting up payment reminders

## Branch 4 — Daily Independence
- Building a basic grocery list
- Planning simple meals
- Reducing eating out
- Comparing cost per meal
- Scheduling doctor and dentist appointments
- Understanding an insurance card
- Reading and questioning a bill before paying
- Applying for a first credit card responsibly
- Understanding credit basics

## Branch 5 — Bigger Decisions
- Understanding rent affordability (rent should not exceed 30% of take-home pay)
- Calculating full move-in cost
- Understanding cosigners for apartments
- Roommate considerations
- Reviewing a lease for red flags
- Asking landlord questions before signing
- Buying a car: affordability, total cost vs monthly payment
- Car cosigners and down payments
- Understanding car loan APR and term length
- Getting a pre-purchase inspection
- Car maintenance: oil changes, tires, warning lights
- Understanding credit scores and reports
- Checking credit reports for errors

## Branch 6 — Career Ready
- Preparing for job interviews
- Practicing common interview questions
- Following up after interviews
- Reviewing a job offer before accepting
- Understanding pay, schedule, benefits, and noncompete clauses
- Moving from first job to career-level job
- Reviewing important documents before signing
- Knowing when to call a professional

## Branch 7 — Soaring
- Emergency savings in place (3 months of expenses is the goal)
- Budget working consistently
- Credit score building on track
- Regular health and dental checkups scheduled
- Decisions made from facts, not panic or pressure
- Exercise, rest, recreation, and mental health as ongoing habits
- Knowing when and how to ask for professional help

---

## Coaching Priority When Overwhelmed

When a user does not know where to start, coach the most urgent milestone first.

Priority order:
1. Safety
2. Housing stability
3. Food
4. Transportation to work or school
5. Income
6. Banking access
7. Bills and debt
8. Credit protection
9. Document review before signing or paying
10. Long-term planning
